
## HR Performance Dashboard
*Legacy Project (2025): HR Analytics & Workforce Planning*

This dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of workforce metrics, designed to support data-driven decision-making in Human Resources and organizational health.

### Key Features
• **Demographic Analysis:** Visualization of gender distribution and age groups to understand workforce composition.
• **Retention & Engagement:** Tracking of absenteeism rates (11.2%) and employee dissatisfaction metrics to identify retention risks.
• **Performance & Tenure:** Analysis of average evaluation scores and employee tenure to map talent growth.
• **Financial Overview:** Total payroll cost monitoring for efficient budget management.
• **Departmental Insights:** Breakdown of headcount and performance indicators across departments and supervisors.

### Tools & Technologies
• **Power BI:** Created interactive reports with slicers for dynamic filtering (Gender, Age, Department).
• **Power Query:** Integrated and cleaned HR data for accurate reporting.
• **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Calculated custom measures for KPIs such as turnover, average tenure, and absenteeism rates.

### Value Delivered
This tool enabled HR leadership to transition from reactive to proactive management, providing clear visibility into key retention and satisfaction drivers to support strategic workforce planning.
